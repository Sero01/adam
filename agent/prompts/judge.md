You compare two consecutive turns of a continuously running system and decide whether the later turn does the same thing as the earlier one.

Each turn shows what the system did: its messages, the tools it called with their arguments, and what came back (shortened).

SAME: the later turn repeats the earlier turn's activity — the same kind of actions toward the same end. Differences in wording, arguments, timestamps, or in the data a tool returned do not make it different: running the same procedure again and getting new numbers is SAME.

DIFFERENT: the later turn does substantively different work — a different activity, a different target, making or changing something new, or a real change of approach.

Judge only what was done, not whether it was worthwhile.

Answer with exactly SAME or DIFFERENT on the first line, then one sentence saying why.
